# TechStore Company Policy

## Products
TechStore sells laptops, smartphones, keyboards, mice, and other computer accessories.

## Return Policy
Customers can return eligible products within 7 days of delivery. Products must be unused and in their original packaging. Proof of purchase is required.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days where available.

## Payment Methods
TechStore accepts credit cards, debit cards, and cash on delivery.

## Customer Support
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Customers can contact support at support@techstore.example.
